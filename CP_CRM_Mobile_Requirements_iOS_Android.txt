1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & CORE OBJECTIVE
================================================================================
CP CRM (Light Edition) is an ultra-fast, field-optimized mobile application designed 
specifically for Channel Partners (Realtors, Real Estate Brokers, Agency Heads, and 
Sub-Agents).

Traditional enterprise real estate CRMs fail in the field because they impose heavy 
bureaucratic friction (OTP verification for leads, complex commission claims, nested 
corporate approval matrices). CP CRM removes these obstacles, providing a high-velocity 
tool focused purely on deal generation and partner enablement.

Key Value Drivers:
- Zero-Friction Deal Flow: No OTPs required for lead submission or site visits.
- On-Demand Collaterals: 1-Tap co-branded PDF brochures with broker contact info.
- Real-Time Inventory: Live tower and unit availability (Available/Blocked/Sold).
- Digital Site Visit Passes: Instant QR code generation for smooth site reception.
- Offline-First Architecture: Uninterrupted operation in basements and low-network areas.

2. SCOPE DEFINITION: INCLUSIONS VS. EXCLUSIONS
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[EXCLUDED MODULES - PURPOSELY OMITTED FOR AGILITY]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. NO COMMISSIONS / PAYOUT WORKFLOWS:
   - Omitted: Commission slabs, tiered percentages, payout requests, GST invoices, 
     TDS calculations, bank account verification.
   - Rationale: Commission settlement is a backend accounting function handled by 
     corporate ERPs. Removing it eliminates UI clutter and keeps the mobile app 
     fast and deal-focused.

2. NO OTP (ONE-TIME PASSWORD) FRICTION:
   - Omitted: SMS OTP on lead entry, SMS OTP at site visit reception desks (GRE), 
     OTP login barriers.
   - Rationale: High-net-worth clients find OTP requests intrusive during casual 
     sales discussions. Protection against duplicates is handled algorithmically.

3. NO CPSM (CHANNEL PARTNER SOURCING MANAGER) APPROVAL BOTTLENECKS:
   - Omitted: CPSM assignment hierarchies, mandatory in-person GPS selfie verification, 
     and multi-tier CP activation gates.
   - Rationale: Brokers should be active within 2 minutes of installing the app.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[INCLUDED MODULES - FULL MOBILE IMPLEMENTATION]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Frictionless Auth & Biometric Unlock (Face ID / Fingerprint)
2. Executive Mobile Dashboard with Real-Time Conversion KPIs
3. Instant Lead Registration with Algorithmic Duplicate Protection
4. Project Catalog with Live Interactive Unit Inventory (Floor/Tower Map)
5. Dynamic Co-Branded Marketing Collateral Generator & 1-Tap Share Sheet
6. QR Site Visit Pass (Instant Reception Scan - Zero OTP)
7. Builder SPOC (Single Point of Contact) Directory & 24h Unit Hold Requests
8. Targeted Push Notifications with Deep Linking
9. Offline Data Caching & Offline Lead Queueing

3.1 iOS SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS & ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Target OS Version: iOS 15.0 and above (iPhone 8 through iPhone 16 Pro Max)

A. UI/UX & Platform Conventions (Apple Human Interface Guidelines):
   - Navigation: Smooth UINavigationController transitions, interactive swipe-to-go-back gesture.
   - Top Bar & Dynamic Island: Proper handling of UIWindowInsets, Dynamic Island, 
     Notch, and Status Bar across all iPhone models.
   - Bottom Sheet / Modals: Cupertino-style modal sheets (`showCupertinoModalPopup` 
     or custom draggable bottom sheets with drag indicator pills).
   - Haptics: Contextual feedback via `UIFeedbackGenerator` (selection, light impact on 
     swipes, success notification haptic on lead submission).
   - Text Inputs: Minimum font size 16pt to prevent unwanted WebKit viewport scaling.
   - System Actions: Pull-to-refresh (`CupertinoSliverRefreshControl`).

B. Biometric Authentication:
   - LocalAuthentication framework: Face ID & Touch ID integration.
   - Mandatory Fallback: Device passcode or account password if biometrics fail.
   - Keychain Integration: Encrypted storage of JWT access and refresh tokens.

C. Native Integrations & URL Schemes:
   - iOS Native Share Sheet (`UIActivityViewController` via `share_plus`) for PDFs, 
     images, and text links.
   - AirDrop compatibility for sharing co-branded marketing brochures directly to clients' Apple devices.
   - URL Schemes (Info.plist `LSApplicationQueriesSchemes`):
     - `whatsapp` (for opening WhatsApp / WhatsApp Business)
     - `tel` (for launching native iPhone dialer)
     - `mailto` (for default Mail app)
     - `sms` (for native Messages app)

D. Push Notifications (APNs):
   - Apple Push Notification service (APNs) combined with Firebase Messaging.
   - Background push mode enabled (`UIBackgroundModes: remote-notification`).
   - Rich notifications with image previews for project launches.

E. iOS Permissions Configuration (`ios/Runner/Info.plist`):
   ```xml
   <key>NSCameraUsageDescription</key>
   <string>CP CRM requires camera access to capture RERA certificates and business cards.</string>
   <key>NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription</key>
   <string>CP CRM requires photo library access to upload documents and agency logos.</string>
   <key>NSFaceIDUsageDescription</key>
   <string>CP CRM uses Face ID for fast and secure biometric login.</string>
   <key>LSApplicationQueriesSchemes</key>
   <array>
       <string>whatsapp</string>
       <string>tel</string>
       <string>mailto</string>
       <string>sms</string>
       <string>https</string>
       <string>http</string>
   </array>
   ```

3.2 ANDROID SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS & ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Target OS Version: Android API Level 26 (Android 8.0 Oreo) up to API Level 35 (Android 15)

A. UI/UX & Platform Conventions (Material Design 3):
   - Visual Style: Material You / M3 with rounded corners (16dp-24dp), elevation 
     overlays, tonal palettes.
   - Navigation: Support for standard Android 3-button navigation bar as well as 
     modern 2-button / gesture navigation bar with transparent window insets (edge-to-edge).
   - Back Navigation: Predictable Back Navigation support (Android 13/14+).
   - Bottom Navigation: Material 3 NavigationBar with badge indicators for leads/updates.
   - Haptics: Standard Android HapticFeedbackConstants on button taps and list swipe actions.

B. Biometric Authentication:
   - Android BiometricPrompt API (supporting Class 3 Strong biometrics - Fingerprint, 
     Face Unlock, Iris).
   - Android Keystore System: Hardware-backed AES encrypted token storage.

C. Native Intent Integrations:
   - Android Intent Chooser (`ACTION_SEND` and `ACTION_SEND_MULTIPLE`) with direct share targets.
   - Direct Intent triggers for `com.whatsapp`, `com.whatsapp.w4b`, native Telephony/Dialer, 
     and SMS managers.

D. Push Notifications (FCM & Notification Channels):
   - Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) integration.
   - Explicit Android Notification Channels:
     - Channel 1: `cp_lead_alerts` (Importance: HIGH, Sound, Vibration) -> For real-time lead status updates.
     - Channel 2: `cp_visit_reminders` (Importance: HIGH) -> For 1-hour site visit countdowns.
     - Channel 3: `cp_broadcasts` (Importance: DEFAULT) -> For new tower pre-launches and pricing alerts.
   - Android 13+ (API 33) Runtime Permission: `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` request flow.

E. Android Manifest & Permissions Configuration (`android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml`):
   ```xml

       <package android:name="com.whatsapp"/>
       <package android:name="com.whatsapp.w4b"/>

           <action android:name="android.intent.action.DIAL"/>
           <data android:scheme="tel"/>

       <intent>
   ```

3.3 COMMON CROSS-PLATFORM DESIGN & THUMB-ZONE ERGONOMICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Bottom Thumb-Zone Layout: All critical primary touchpoints (Call, Add Lead, 
  Share Kit, Book Visit) are positioned within the lower 40% of the mobile screen.
- Minimum Touch Target: 48dp x 48dp for all interactive buttons, chips, and icons.
- List Swipe Actions:
  - Swipe Right: Direct native call trigger (`tel:`)
  - Swipe Left: Direct pre-filled WhatsApp message trigger (`wa.me`)
- Dark & Light Theme Support: Dynamic theme switching based on device system settings. 
  Dark mode uses OLED-friendly deep slate/black backgrounds (`#121212` / `#1E1E2E`).

4. FUNCTIONAL MODULE REQUIREMENTS (STEP-BY-STEP)
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 MODULE 1: FAST AUTHENTICATION, ONBOARDING & PARTNER PROFILE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Objective: Allow brokers to install the app and begin submitting leads in under 2 minutes.

Requirements:
1. Registration Screen:
   - Partner Type Toggle: [ Individual Realtor ] vs [ Real Estate Agency / Firm ]
   - Fields:
     - Full Name / Firm Name (Required, Text)
     - RERA Registration Number (Required, Alphanumeric uppercase formatting)
     - Primary Mobile Number (Required, 10-digit validation)
     - Email Address (Required, Email format)
     - City & State (Dropdown selection)
     - Password (Minimum 8 chars)
   - Optional Quick KYC: In-app camera capture or gallery selection of RERA Certificate 
     and Business Card. (Can be skipped during registration; prompted before unit booking).
   - Instant Account Activation: Upon form submission, account is activated immediately. 
     No waiting for physical broker meetings.

2. Login Screen:
   - Mobile Number or Email + Password login.
   - "Enable Biometric Login" toggle prompt on first successful login.
   - Biometric Quick Login button (Face ID icon on iOS, Fingerprint icon on Android).
   - Silent JWT token refresh mechanism without forcing the user to re-login.

3. Sub-Broker & Team Management (for Agency Accounts):
   - Agency Admin can generate invite links or add team member mobile numbers.
   - Sub-brokers log into their own app instance; all submitted leads and visits are 
     automatically tagged with the parent agency's RERA and identifier.

4. Profile & Co-Branding Setup:
   - Edit contact details, upload high-resolution Firm Logo, and set office address.
   - Preview generated co-branded business card and watermark badge.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.2 MODULE 2: EXECUTIVE MOBILE DASHBOARD & REAL-TIME FUNNEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Objective: Give partners an instant executive overview of their pipeline and actions.

Requirements:
1. Header Bar:
   - Partner Profile Avatar & Agency Name.
   - Quick search icon and notification bell with unread badge counter.

   - Card 1: [ Active Leads ] (Count of open client conversations)

3. Pipeline Conversion Funnel:
   - Horizontal interactive progress bar showing breakdown:
     [ New ] -> [ Contacted ] -> [ Visit Fixed ] -> [ Visit Done ] -> [ Token/Booked ]

4. Quick Action Floating Dock (Fixed at bottom thumb-zone):

4.3 MODULE 3: INSTANT LEAD CAPTURE & ZERO-OTP DUPLICATE PROTECTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Objective: Capture client details in under 20 seconds with foolproof conflict resolution.

Requirements:
1. Fast Lead Entry Form:
   - Client First & Last Name (Required)
   - Primary Phone Number (Required, 10-digit auto-formatted)
   - Alternative Phone Number (Optional)
   - Email Address (Optional)
   - Budget Range Selector (Chips: ₹40L-₹70L | ₹70L-₹1Cr | ₹1Cr-₹2Cr | ₹2Cr+)
   - Preferred Configuration (Multi-select checkboxes: 1 BHK | 2 BHK | 3 BHK | 4 BHK | Villa/Plot)
   - Target Project (Searchable dropdown of active builder projects)
   - Expected Purchase Timeline (Chips: Immediate <15 days | 30 Days | 90 Days | Exploring)
   - Specific Client Notes (Free text, e.g., "Prefers East-facing higher floor, Vastu compliant")

2. Algorithmic Duplicate Protection (Zero-OTP Client Verification):
   - Real-time phone number deduplication against backend database on entry.
   - Case A (New Lead): Instant confirmation with green badge -> Lead registered under partner RERA.
   - Case B (Duplicate within Active Window, e.g., 60 days): Clear warning dialog:
     *"This contact is already active with another partner or visited directly on [Date]. Your registration cannot be accepted at this time."*
   - Zero OTP required from the client, eliminating drop-offs and broker friction.

3. Lead Detail View & Lifecycle Tracker:
   - Current stage badge: `NEW` | `CONTACTED` | `VISIT_SCHEDULED` | `VISIT_COMPLETED` | `TOKEN_PAID` | `LOST`
   - Complete interaction history timeline with timestamped notes.
   - 1-Tap Field Communication Actions:
     - [ Call Client ]: Launches native phone dialer with number pre-dialed (`tel:XXXXXXXXXX`).
     - [ WhatsApp ]: Opens WhatsApp conversation with pre-composed greeting and project deck.
     - [ SMS ]: Opens native SMS composer.

4. Filter, Search & Sorting:
   - Live search by client name or phone number.
   - Filter chips: By Project, By Stage, By Budget, and By Date Range.

6. BACKEND REST API INTEGRATION CONTRACTS
================================================================================

The mobile app communicates with the backend REST APIs using the following endpoints:

1. POST /api/auth/token/
   - Description: Authenticate CP with mobile/email + password.
   - Response: `{ "access": "JWT_ACCESS_TOKEN", "refresh": "JWT_REFRESH_TOKEN", "user": { "id": 101, "name": "Rahul Sharma", "firm": "Apex Realty", "rera": "RERA12345" } }`

2. POST /api/auth/register-cp/
   - Description: Self-registration for Individual or Agency brokers.
   - Payload: `{ "type": "AGENCY", "firm_name": "Apex Realty", "rera_number": "RERA12345", "mobile": "9876543210", "email": "rahul@apex.com", "city": "Bangalore", "password": "..." }`
   - Response: `{ "status": "SUCCESS", "message": "Account created and activated." }`

3. GET /api/cp/dashboard-light/
   - Description: Fetch real-time dashboard KPIs, active funnel, and announcements.
   - Response: `{ "kpis": { "active_leads": 42, "visits_scheduled": 8, "visits_completed": 35, "closures": 5 }, "funnel": { "new": 12, "contacted": 15, "visit_fixed": 8, "visit_done": 4, "booked": 3 }, "announcements": [...] }`

4. GET /api/cp/leads/
   - Description: Fetch all leads registered by the CP (supports search, stage, and project filters).

7. OFFLINE DATA SYNC, CACHING & PERFORMANCE STRATEGY
================================================================================

Real estate sales executives and channel partners frequently operate in underground 
parking garages, remote construction sites, and concrete basements with poor cellular signal.

Offline Strategy:
1. Lead Submission Queue (Offline-First):
   - If network connectivity is lost (`connectivity_plus == none`), new lead submissions 
     are saved to a local Hive queue with status `QUEUED_OFFLINE`.
   - A background sync service monitors network status and flushes queued leads as soon 
     as an active internet connection is detected.
   - Visual indicator on the Lead screen shows "1 lead waiting to sync".

2. Project Catalog & Floor Plan Caching:
   - High-resolution floor plans and project brochures are cached locally on first view 
     using `cached_network_image` and `path_provider`.
   - Brokers can present layouts to buyers without experiencing loading spinners or network timeouts.

3. Image & PDF Optimization:
   - App client requests WebP / compressed thumbnails for catalog browsing, downloading 
     full-res PDFs only on explicit download/share actions.


================================================================================
8. SECURITY, PRIVACY & DEVICE PERMISSIONS MATRIX
================================================================================

1. Zero-OTP Security Model:
   - OTP elimination is balanced by strict cryptographic server-side deduplication rules 
     and timestamp hashing to prevent broker dispute exploits.

2. Sensitive Token Security:
   - JWT tokens are stored exclusively in the iOS Keychain and Android Keystore 
     via `flutter_secure_storage`.
   - Plaintext credentials or tokens are never stored in SharedPreferences or NSUserDefaults.

3. Privacy & OS Permissions:
   - Camera: Used exclusively for document upload (RERA certificate & business card).
   - Photos/Gallery: Used for selecting existing files/logos.
   - Biometrics: Used strictly for local device authentication; biometric biometric data 
     never leaves the local device Secure Enclave / TEE.
